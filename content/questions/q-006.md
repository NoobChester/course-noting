Expand $(4 + i)(5 + 3i)$ and hence show that $\frac{\pi}{4} = \arctan \frac{1}{4} + \arctan \frac{3}{5}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$(4+i)(5+3i) = 20 + 12i + 5i - 3 = 17 + 17i = 17(1+i)$. Taking the argument of both sides gives $\arg(4+i) + \arg(5+3i) = \arg(17(1+i)) = \frac{\pi}{4}$.
