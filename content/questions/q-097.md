### Prompt
Define the function $f$ by $f(z) = \frac{3}{(z - 2)(z + 1)}$. Find all Laurent expansions of $f$ in powers of $z-1$ and state the region of convergence of each expansion.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Singularities at $z = 2$ and $z = -1$. In powers of $w = z-1$, poles are at $w = 1$ and $w = -2$. The regions are: (1) $|z-1| < 1$, (2) $1 < |z-1| < 2$, and (3) $|z-1| > 2$.
