### Prompt
Suppose that $f(z) = z e^{-z}$. Write $f(z)$ in the form $u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$, where $z = x + iy$, and in the form $u(r, \theta) + iv(r, \theta)$, where $z = r e^{i\theta}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Cartesian: $u(x,y) = e^{-x}(x \cos y + y \sin y)$, $v(x,y) = e^{-x}(y \cos y - x \sin y)$. Polar: $u(r,\theta) = r e^{-r\cos\theta}\cos(\theta - r\sin\theta)$, $v(r,\theta) = r e^{-r\cos\theta}\sin(\theta - r\sin\theta)$.
