### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: One. On $|z| = 1$, let $f(z) = -8z$. $|f(z)| = 8$. Let $g(z) = z^9 - 2z^6 + z^2 - 2$. $|g(z)| \le 1 + 2 + 1 + 2 = 6 < 8$. By Rouché's theorem, the polynomial has the same number of roots inside $|z| < 1$ as $-8z$, which is 1.
