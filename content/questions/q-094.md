### Prompt
Define $f(z) = \frac{2z - 4}{z^2 - 4z + 3}$. Find the Laurent series for $f(z)$ that converges when $|z - 1| > 2$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{-1} c_n (z - 1)^n$, where $c_{-1} = 2$ and $c_n = 2^{-n-1}$ when $n \le -2$.
