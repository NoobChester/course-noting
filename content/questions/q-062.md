### Prompt
Find all solutions $z \in \mathbb{C}$ of (a) $\cosh z = -1$, (b) $\sin z = 3$, (c) $\sinh z = 2i$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $z = (2k+1)\pi i$.
(b) $\sin z = \frac{e^{iz} - e^{-iz}}{2i} = 3 \implies e^{2iz} - 6i e^{iz} - 1 = 0 \implies e^{iz} = (3 \pm 2\sqrt{2})i$.
(c) $\sinh z = i \sin(-iz) = 2i \implies \sin(y) = 2$ with $x=0$, so $z = \pm \cosh^{-1}(2) + (2k+1/2)\pi i$.
