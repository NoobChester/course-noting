### Prompt
Show that $\int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{dx}{(x^2 + a^2)(x^2 + b^2)} = \frac{\pi}{ab(a + b)}$ when $a, b > 0$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Poles in upper half plane at $z = ia$ and $z = ib$. Residues are $\frac{1}{2ia(b^2-a^2)}$ and $\frac{1}{2ib(a^2-b^2)}$. Summing and multiplying by $2\pi i$ yields $\frac{\pi}{ab(a+b)}$.
