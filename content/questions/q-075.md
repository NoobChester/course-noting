### Prompt
(a) Find $\int_\gamma (e^z + z) \, dz$, where $\gamma(t) = 1 + i\pi t$ for $0 \le t \le 1$.
(b) Show that $\int_\gamma \operatorname{Log} z \, dz = -2i$, where $\gamma(t) = e^{i\pi t}$ for $-1/2 \le t \le 1/2$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $e^z + z$ has primitive $e^z + z^2/2$. Evaluating at end points gives $(e^{1+i\pi} + (1+i\pi)^2/2) - (e^1 + 1/2) = -e + (1+2i\pi-\pi^2)/2 - e - 1/2 = -2e - \pi^2/2 + i\pi$.
(b) Primitive is $z \operatorname{Log} z - z$.
