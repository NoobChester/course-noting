### Official Answer / Solution Notes
If $f = u + iv$ is real-valued, $v(x,y) = 0$ everywhere. By CR equations, $u_x = v_y = 0$ and $u_y = -v_x = 0$. Since both partial derivatives of $u$ vanish everywhere on connected $\mathbb{C}$, $u$ is constant, so $f$ is constant.
