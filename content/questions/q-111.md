### Prompt
Evaluate the integral $\int_\gamma \frac{e^z}{\sin(z) + i \sinh(z)} \, dz$ where $\gamma(t) = e^{i2\pi t}, 0 \le t \le 1$.
What is the radius of the largest circle centred at the origin for which the integral has the same value?

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
At $z = 0$, $\sin z + i \sinh z = 2z + O(z^3)$. Thus $z = 0$ is a simple pole with residue $\lim_{z \to 0} z \frac{e^z}{2z} = 1/2$. The integral around $|z| = 1$ is $2\pi i(1/2) = \pi i$. The integral remains $\pi i$ until the circle reaches the next zero of $\sin z + i \sinh z$.
