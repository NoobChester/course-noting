### Prompt
Find the Taylor series for $f(z) = (1+z)^{1/2}$ in powers of $z$ in the disc $|z| < 1$. Use the branch of the square root that is equal to $+1$ when $z = 0$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: $\sum_{n=0}^\infty c_n z^n$, where $c_0 = 1, c_1 = 1/2$ and $c_n = \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n! 2^n}(2n - 3)!!$ when $n > 1$.
