### Prompt
Find the image of $\{z \in \mathbb{C} : 0 < \operatorname{Re}(z) < \pi/2, \operatorname{Im} z > 0\}$ under the mappings:
(a) $w = \cos z$, (b) $w = \cos^2 z$, (c) $w = \cos^{1/2} z$.
(Use the principal branch of the square root.)

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $w = \cos(x+iy) = \cos x \cosh y - i \sin x \sinh y$. Since $0 < x < \pi/2$ and $y > 0$, $u > 0$ and $v < 0$, which maps to the fourth quadrant $\{w : \operatorname{Re}(w) > 0, \operatorname{Im}(w) < 0\}$.
