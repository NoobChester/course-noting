### Prompt
Suppose that $\alpha = \cos t + i\sin t$ where $0 < t < 2\pi$. Show that $\frac{1 + \alpha}{1 - \alpha} = i \cot \frac{t}{2}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Since $\alpha = e^{it}$, we have $\frac{1 + e^{it}}{1 - e^{it}} = \frac{e^{it/2}(e^{-it/2} + e^{it/2})}{e^{it/2}(e^{-it/2} - e^{it/2})} = \frac{2 \cos(t/2)}{-2i \sin(t/2)} = i \cot(t/2)$.
