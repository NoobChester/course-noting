### Prompt
Find the integral $\int_0^\infty \frac{x^q}{x^p + 1} \, dx$, where $p, q \in \mathbb{R}^+$ and $p > q + 1$, without using complex methods.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Substitute $u = x^p, du = p x^{p-1} dx$, transforming into the Beta integral $B\left(\frac{q+1}{p}, 1 - \frac{q+1}{p}\right) = \frac{\pi}{\sin(\pi(q+1)/p)}$.
