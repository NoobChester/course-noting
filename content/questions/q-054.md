### Prompt
Suppose that $v$ is a harmonic conjugate of $u$. Show that $-u$ is a harmonic conjugate of $v$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
If $v$ is conjugate of $u$, then $f = u + iv$ is holomorphic. Multiplying by $-i$, $-i f = v - iu = v + i(-u)$ is also holomorphic. Thus $-u$ is a harmonic conjugate of $v$.
