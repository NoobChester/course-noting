### Prompt
(a) Show that $|z_1 + z_2|^2 + |z_1 - z_2|^2 = 2|z_1|^2 + 2|z_2|^2$ for all $z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{C}$.
(b) Give a geometrical interpretation of this result.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) Use $|w|^2 = w \bar{w}$. Expanding both terms causes cross-terms to cancel. (b) In any parallelogram, the sum of the squares of the lengths of the two diagonals equals the sum of the squares of the lengths of the four sides.
