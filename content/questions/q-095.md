### Prompt
Define $f(z) = (z - 2i)^{-1} - (z + i)^{-1}$. Find the Laurent series for $f$ with centre $1$ that converges at $3$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Singularities are at $2i$ (distance $|1-2i| = \sqrt{5}$) and $-i$ (distance $|1+i| = \sqrt{2}$). At $z = 3$, distance is $|3-1| = 2$. Since $\sqrt{2} < 2 < \sqrt{5}$, we expand in the annulus $\sqrt{2} < |z - 1| < \sqrt{5}$.
