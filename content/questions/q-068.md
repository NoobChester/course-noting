### Prompt
Write $z = r e^{i\theta}$. Find $\operatorname{Re}(z^i)$, $\operatorname{Im}(z^i)$, $|z^i|$ in terms of $r$ and $\theta$. (Use the principal value of $z^i$.)

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$z^i = e^{i \operatorname{Log} z} = e^{i(\ln r + i\theta)} = e^{-\theta} e^{i \ln r}$. Thus $|z^i| = e^{-\theta}$, $\operatorname{Re}(z^i) = e^{-\theta}\cos(\ln r)$, $\operatorname{Im}(z^i) = e^{-\theta}\sin(\ln r)$.
