### Official Answer / Solution Notes
For $|z|$: by reverse triangle inequality, $||z| - |z_0|| \le |z - z_0| < \delta = \epsilon$. For $\operatorname{Re}(z)$: $|\operatorname{Re}(z) - \operatorname{Re}(z_0)| = |\operatorname{Re}(z - z_0)| \le |z - z_0| < \delta = \epsilon$.
