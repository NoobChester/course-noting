### Prompt
How many roots of $z^4 - 8z + 10 = 0$ lie in the annulus $1 < |z| < 3$? How many of these lie in the first quadrant?

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: Four; one. On $|z| = 1$, dominant term is $10 > |z^4 - 8z| = 9$, so $0$ roots inside $|z| < 1$. On $|z| = 3$, dominant term is $z^4$ ($3^4 = 81 > |8z-10| = 34$), so all $4$ roots lie in $|z| < 3$. Hence all 4 roots lie in $1 < |z| < 3$.
