### Prompt
Let $\gamma$ be the unit circle $\{e^{i\theta} : -\pi \le \theta \le \pi\}$. Use the Cauchy integral formula to find $\int_\gamma e^z z^n \, dz$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
If $n \ge 0$, $e^z z^n$ is entire, so the integral is $0$. If $n = -k$ with $k \ge 1$, $\int_\gamma \frac{e^z}{z^k} dz = \frac{2\pi i}{(k-1)!} \left.\frac{d^{k-1}}{dz^{k-1}} e^z\right|_{z=0} = \frac{2\pi i}{(k-1)!} = \frac{2\pi i}{(-n-1)!}$.
