### Prompt
Use the Laplace transform to find the solutions $u, v : [0,\infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ to the integral equations:
$$u(t) = e^{-t} + \int_0^t (t - s) e^{s-t} u(s) \, ds$$
and
$$v(t) = e^{-t} + \int_0^t (t - s)^2 v(s) \, ds.$$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Notice the integrals are convolutions! For $u$: $U(z) = \frac{1}{z+1} + \frac{1}{(z+1)^2} U(z) \implies U(z) = \frac{z+1}{(z+1)^2 - 1} = \frac{z+1}{z(z+2)} = \frac{1/2}{z} + \frac{1/2}{z+2}$. Inverting yields $u(t) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}e^{-2t}$.
