### Prompt
Without finding the series, state the radius of convergence of the Taylor series for the function $f$ given by $f(z) = \frac{\cos z}{z(z + 1)}$ about the point $z = 1 + i$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Singularities of $f$ are at $z = 0$ and $z = -1$. Distance to $0$ is $|(1+i) - 0| = \sqrt{2}$. Distance to $-1$ is $|(1+i) - (-1)| = |2+i| = \sqrt{5}$. The nearest singularity is at $0$, so $R = \sqrt{2}$.
