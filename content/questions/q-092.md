### Prompt
Define the function $f$ by $f(z) = \frac{z}{2} + \frac{z}{e^z - 1}$ for $z \ne 0$ and $f(0) = 1$. Show that $f$ has a Maclaurin series of the form $a_0 + a_2 z^2 + a_4 z^4 + \dots$, and find $a_0, a_2$ and $a_4$.
Note: The numbers $B_{2n}$ defined as $B_{2n} = a_{2n}(2n)!$ are known as the Bernoulli numbers.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$f(z) = \frac{z}{2} \frac{e^z + 1}{e^z - 1} = \frac{z}{2} \coth(z/2)$, which is an even function! Therefore all odd coefficients vanish. $a_0 = 1, a_2 = 1/12, a_4 = -1/720$.
