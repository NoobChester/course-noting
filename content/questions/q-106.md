### Prompt
Use complex analysis methods to show that:
(a) $\int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\cos 2\theta}{5 + 4 \cos \theta} \, d\theta = \frac{\pi}{6}$
(b) $\int_0^{2\pi} \frac{\sin^2 \theta}{5 + 3 \cos \theta} \, d\theta = \frac{2\pi}{9}$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Substitute $z = e^{i\theta}, \cos\theta = \frac{z+z^{-1}}{2}, d\theta = \frac{dz}{iz}$ around $|z| = 1$. The only pole inside $|z| < 1$ is at $z = -1/2$ (or $-1/3$). Calculating residues gives $\pi/6$ and $2\pi/9$.
