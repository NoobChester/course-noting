### Prompt
Define functions $f$ and $g$ by $f(z) = \operatorname{Log}(iz)$ and $g(z) = z^{-1} \operatorname{Log}(z + 1)$.
(a) Where are $f$ and $g$ holomorphic?
(b) Find a branch of $\log(iz)$ which is holomorphic in the region $\{z : \operatorname{Im}(z) > 0\}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: (a) $f$ is holomorphic except when $z = iy$, where $y \ge 0$; while $g$ is holomorphic except when $z = 0$ or $z = x$, where $x \le -1$. (b) $i\pi/2 + \operatorname{Log}(z)$.
