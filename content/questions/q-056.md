### Prompt
Find the centre and radius of convergence of the following power series; if possible, identify the sum:
(a) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty (z - 2)^n$
(b) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{3^n} (z - 2)^n$
(c) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{n!} (z - 2)^n$
(d) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{1}{3^n n!} (z - 2)^n$
(e) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty n(z - 2)^n$
(f) $\sum_{n=0}^\infty n^2(z - 2)^n$
(g) $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n} (z - 2)^n$
(h) $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n(n + 1)} (z - 2)^n$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
All series are centred at $z_0 = 2$.
(a) $R = 1$, sum = $\frac{1}{1 - (z-2)} = \frac{1}{3-z}$.
(b) $R = 3$, sum = $\frac{3}{5-z}$.
(c) $R = \infty$, sum = $e^{z-2}$.
(d) $R = \infty$, sum = $e^{(z-2)/3}$.
(e) $R = 1$, sum = $\frac{z-2}{(3-z)^2}$.
(g) $R = 1$, sum = $-\operatorname{Log}(3-z)$.
