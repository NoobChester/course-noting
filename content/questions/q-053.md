### Prompt
Using the result of the previous question to guess $f$, or otherwise, show that the following are harmonic and find holomorphic functions $f$ of which they are the real parts:
(a) $x - x^3 + 3xy^2$,
(b) $\cos x \cosh y$,
(c) $\frac{x}{x^2 + y^2}$, where $(x, y) \ne (0, 0)$,
*(d) $\frac{x(x^2 + y^2 + 1)}{(x^2 + y^2)^2 + 2(x^2 - y^2) + 1}$, where $(x, y) \ne \pm(0, 1)$,
(e) $y$.
