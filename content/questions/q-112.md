### Prompt
Let $f$ be an entire function, and take $a, b \in \mathbb{C}$. For $R > \max\{|a|, |b|\}$, estimate $\int_{|z|=R} \frac{f(z)}{(z - a)(z - b)} \, dz$.
Assuming also that $f$ is bounded, let $R \to \infty$ and show that $f$ is constant.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
By Cauchy's integral formula, for any $R$, $\int_{|z|=R} \frac{f(z)}{(z-a)(z-b)} dz = 2\pi i \frac{f(a) - f(b)}{a - b}$. On the circle $|z|=R$, $|f(z)| \le M$, so the integral is bounded by $2\pi R \frac{M}{(R-|a|)(R-|b|)} \to 0$ as $R \to \infty$. Thus $f(a) - f(b) = 0 \implies f(a) = f(b)$ for all $a, b$.
