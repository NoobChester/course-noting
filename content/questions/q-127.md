### Prompt
Find the functions whose Laplace transforms are given by:
(a) $\frac{1}{z(z + 1)}$
(b) $\frac{z + 1}{z^2(z - 1)}$
(c) $\frac{1}{z^4 + 1}$
(d) $\frac{1}{(z^2 + 1)^2}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $1 - e^{-t}$. (b) Partial fractions $\frac{2}{z-1} - \frac{2}{z} - \frac{1}{z^2} \implies 2e^t - 2 - t$. (d) $\frac{1}{2}(\sin t - t \cos t)$.
