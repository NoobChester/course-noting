### Prompt
Let $\gamma$ be any contour from $1 - i$ to $1 + i$. Evaluate the following integrals:
(a) $\int_\gamma 4z^3 \, dz$;
(b) $\int_\gamma \cos z \, dz$;
(c) $\int_\gamma \sin 2z \, dz$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $[z^4]_{1-i}^{1+i} = (1+i)^4 - (1-i)^4 = -4 - (-4) = 0$.
(b) $[\sin z]_{1-i}^{1+i} = 2i \cos 1 \sinh 1$.
(c) $[-\frac{1}{2}\cos 2z]_{1-i}^{1+i} = i \sin 2 \sinh 2$.
