### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Consider the triangle in the complex plane with vertices at $0$, $-z$, and $w$. The side lengths are $|-z - 0| = |z|$, $|w - (-z)| = |w + z|$, and $|w - 0| = |w|$. By the triangle inequality (the length of one side is at most the sum of the other two), $|w| \le |w+z| + |z|$.
