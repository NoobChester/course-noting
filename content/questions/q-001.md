### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Let $z = x + iy$. Then $e^z = e^{x+iy} = e^x e^{iy}$. Since $|e^{iy}| = |\cos y + i \sin y| = 1$, we have $|e^z| = |e^x| |e^{iy}| = e^x = e^{\operatorname{Re}(z)}$.
