### Prompt
By integrating $f(z) = (1 + z^2)^{-1}$ around the perimeter of the rectangle with corners at $0, a, a+ib$ and $ib$ and letting $a \to \infty$, where $0 < b < 1$, show that
$$\int_0^\infty \frac{1 - b^2 + x^2}{(1 - b^2 + x^2)^2 + 4b^2x^2} \, dx = \frac{\pi}{2},$$
and
$$\int_0^\infty \frac{x}{(1 - b^2 + x^2)^2 + 4b^2x^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{4b} \operatorname{Log}\frac{1+b}{1-b}.$$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$f(z) = \frac{1}{1+z^2}$ has poles at $\pm i$. Since $0 < b < 1$, no pole lies inside the rectangle, so $\oint f(z) dz = 0$. Equating real and imaginary parts of the horizontal and vertical segments gives the two integrals.
