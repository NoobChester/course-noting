### Prompt
Where are the following functions differentiable? Where are they holomorphic?
(a) $f_1(z) = z|z|^2$;
(b) $f_2(x + iy) = x^2 + iy^2$;
(c) $f_3(x + iy) = (xy^2 + 5) + i(y - x^2y - 3)$;
(d) $f_4(x + iy) = (3xy^2 + 6x^2 - 4x + 3) + i(3y^2 - 3x^2y + 2y + 15)$;
(e) $f_5(z) = e^z(z - \bar{z})^2$;
(f) $f_6(x + iy) = |x| + i|y|$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: (a) $f_1$ is differentiable at $0$; (b) $f_2$ is differentiable on the line $x = y$; (c) $f_3$ is differentiable on $x^2+y^2=1$; (d) $f_4$ on $(x+2)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 7$; (e) $f_5$ on the $x$-axis ($y=0$); (f) $f_6$ when $xy > 0$.
$f_1$ to $f_5$ are differentiable only on curves/points containing no open set, so they are nowhere holomorphic! $f_6$ is holomorphic on the open quadrants $xy > 0$.
