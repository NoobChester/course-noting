### Fundamental Theorems of Vector Calculus

1. **Green's Theorem:** $\oint_C P dx + Q dy = \iint_D (Q_x - P_y) dA$
2. **Stokes' Theorem:** $\oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = \iint_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot d\mathbf{S}$
3. **Divergence Theorem:** $\iint_S \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \iiint_E (\nabla \cdot \mathbf{F}) dV$
