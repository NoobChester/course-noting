### Prompt
Suppose that $f(z) = z^3 - z + 1$. Write $f(z)$ in the form $u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$, where $z = x + iy$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
u(x, y) = x^3 - 3xy^2 - x + 1 and v(x, y) = 3x^2y - y^3 - y.
