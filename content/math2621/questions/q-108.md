### Prompt
Evaluate $\int_{|z|=1} \frac{z^2}{2z + 1} \, dz$ and hence prove that $\int_0^\pi \frac{2 \cos 2\theta + \cos 3\theta}{5 + 4 \cos \theta} \, d\theta = \frac{\pi}{8}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Residue of $\frac{z^2}{2z+1}$ at $z = -1/2$ is $(-1/2)^2/2 = 1/8$, so the contour integral is $2\pi i(1/8) = \pi i / 4$. Parameterizing $z = e^{i\theta}$ and equating real parts produces $\pi/8$.
