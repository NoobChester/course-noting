### Prompt
Find $\frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_\gamma \frac{\sin \pi z}{(z - 1)^n} \, dz$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}$ and $\gamma$ is a simple closed anticlockwise contour around $1$.
