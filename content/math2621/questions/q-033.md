### Prompt
Suppose that $u(x, y) = x^2 + y^2$ and $v(x, y) = x - y$. Can you write $u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$ as a simple function of $z$? Or of $z$ and $\bar{z}$?

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$x^2+y^2 = |z|^2 = z\bar{z}$, and $x = (z+\bar{z})/2$, $y = (z-\bar{z})/(2i)$. Thus $u+iv = z\bar{z} + i\left(\frac{z+\bar{z}}{2} - \frac{z-\bar{z}}{2i}\right) = z\bar{z} + \frac{1+i}{2}z - \frac{1-i}{2}\bar{z}$. Because it depends explicitly on $\bar{z}$, it cannot be written as a function of $z$ alone (it is not holomorphic).
