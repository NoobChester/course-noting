### Prompt
Find $\int_\Gamma \frac{\cos z}{z^2(z^2 + 1)} \, dz$: (a) when $\Gamma$ is the circle $|z| = 2$ and (b) when $\Gamma$ is the circle $|z - i/2| = 1$, both described once anticlockwise.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Poles at $0$ (double), $i$ (simple), $-i$ (simple). Residue at $0$ is $0$ (integrand is even). Residue at $i$ is $\frac{\cos(i)}{i^2(2i)} = \frac{\cosh(1)}{-2i} = \frac{i\cosh(1)}{2}$. For (a), sum of all residues is $0$, so integral = $0$. For (b), only $0$ and $i$ inside: integral = $2\pi i (i\cosh(1)/2) = -\pi \cosh(1)$.
