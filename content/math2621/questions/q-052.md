### Prompt
Suppose $u, v$ are harmonic and satisfy the Cauchy-Riemann equations in $\mathbb{R}^2$. Show that $f = u + iv$ satisfies $f'(x) = u_x(x, 0) - i u_y(x, 0)$ for real $x$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Since $f'(z) = u_x(x,y) + i v_x(x,y) = u_x(x,y) - i u_y(x,y)$ by CR equations. Setting $y = 0$ gives $f'(x) = u_x(x,0) - i u_y(x,0)$.
