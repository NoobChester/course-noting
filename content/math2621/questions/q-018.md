### Official Answer / Solution Notes
If $P, Q$ are polynomials, $P(Q(z)) = \sum a_k (Q(z))^k$ is a finite sum of products of polynomials, hence a polynomial. If $R_1 = P_1/Q_1$ and $R_2 = P_2/Q_2$, substituting $R_2$ into $R_1$ and multiplying numerator and denominator by $Q_2(z)^{\deg P_1}$ yields a quotient of two polynomials.
