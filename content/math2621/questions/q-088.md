### Prompt
Show that $\frac{1}{1 + z + z^2} = \sum_{n=0}^\infty (z^{3n} - z^{3n+1})$ in the disc $|z| < 1$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Notice $\frac{1}{1+z+z^2} = \frac{1-z}{1-z^3} = (1-z) \sum_{n=0}^\infty (z^3)^n = \sum_{n=0}^\infty (z^{3n} - z^{3n+1})$ for $|z^3| < 1 \iff |z| < 1$.
