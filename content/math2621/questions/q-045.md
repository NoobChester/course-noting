### Prompt
Suppose that $f$ is holomorphic in $\Omega$, and $f'$ is continuous; let $g(z) = \overline{f(\bar{z})}$. Is $g$ holomorphic? Where?

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: $f$ satisfies the Cauchy–Riemann equations in $\Omega$, and so $g$ satisfies the Cauchy–Riemann equations in the conjugate set $\bar{\Omega} = \{z : \bar{z} \in \Omega\}$. The partial derivatives are continuous and $\bar{\Omega}$ is open, so $g$ is holomorphic in $\bar{\Omega}$.
