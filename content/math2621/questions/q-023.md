Find the image of the following regions under the mapping $w = z^{-1}$:

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) Circle passing through origin: $u^2 + v^2 - \frac{1}{4}u + \frac{1}{4}v = 0$.
(b) Line $\operatorname{Re}(w) = 1/2$.
(c) Half-plane $\operatorname{Re}(w) \ge 1/2$.
