### Prompt
Show from the definition, and then using the Cauchy Riemann equations, that $f_1(z) = \operatorname{Im}(z)$ and $f_2(x + iy) = 3x + 4iy$ are nowhere differentiable.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
For $f_1(z) = y$, $u = y, v = 0$. $u_x = 0 \ne v_y = 0$, but $u_y = 1 \ne -v_x = 0$. For $f_2$, $u = 3x, v = 4y$, $u_x = 3 \ne v_y = 4$. CR equations fail everywhere.
