### Prompt
Compute the Laplace transforms of each of the following functions, stating the domain of the transform:
(a) $f(t) = e^{-at}$
(b) $f(t) = \cos(\omega t)$
(c) $f(t) = t(t^2 - 1)$,
where $a \in \mathbb{C}$ and $\omega \in \mathbb{R}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) $\frac{1}{z+a}$, domain $\operatorname{Re}(z) > -\operatorname{Re}(a)$. (b) $\frac{z}{z^2+\omega^2}$, domain $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0$. (c) $\mathcal{L}\{t^3 - t\} = \frac{6}{z^4} - \frac{1}{z^2}$, domain $\operatorname{Re}(z) > 0$.
