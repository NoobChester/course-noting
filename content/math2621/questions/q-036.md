### Prompt
Find the following limits, if they exist, or explain why they do not exist:
(a) $\lim_{z \to -1} \operatorname{Arg}(z)$
(b) $\lim_{z \to 1+i} \frac{z^2 - z + 1 - i}{z^2 - 2z + 2}$
(c) $\lim_{z \to 0} e^{1/z}$
(d) $\lim_{z \to 0} e^{1/z^4}$
(e) $\lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2}$
(f) $\lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} \frac{xy^2}{x^2 + y^4}$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: (a) does not exist (discontinuous jump from $\pi$ to $-\pi$ across negative real axis);
(b) $1 - i/2$;
(c) does not exist (approaching $0$ from left vs right yields $0$ vs $\infty$);
(d) does not exist (different rays yield different limits);
(e) does not exist (depends on slope $y = mx$);
(f) does not exist (path $x = my^2$ gives $m/(m^2+1)$).
