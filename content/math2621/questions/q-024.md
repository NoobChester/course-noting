### Prompt
Find the image of:
(a) the region $|z - 1| \le 1$ under the mapping $w = \frac{z}{z + 2}$,
(b) the line $x + 2y = 2$ under the mapping $w = \frac{1}{z + i}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) Since circle passes through $0, 2, 1+i$, test three boundary points. Maps to a disk centered on the real axis.
(b) Straight line maps to a circle passing through $w=0$.
