### Prompt
Define $f(z) = \frac{1}{\sin(z)}$. Find the Laurent series for $f$ up to terms in $z^3$ about $z = 0$ that is valid at $1/2$, and state the region of convergence.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: $z^{-1} + \frac{1}{6}z + \frac{7}{360}z^3 + \dots$, valid for $0 < |z| < \pi$.
