### Prompt
Suppose that a linear transformation $L$ of $\mathbb{R}^2$ preserves angles between lines passing through the origin. Show that $L$ is the composition of a scalar multiplication and a rotation, and possibly a reflection.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Since $L$ preserves angles, it preserves orthogonality. The image of the standard orthonormal basis must be orthogonal with equal lengths, so $L$ is a similarity transformation (scalar multiple of an orthogonal matrix).
