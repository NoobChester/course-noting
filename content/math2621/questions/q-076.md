### Prompt
Show that $\int_\gamma \frac{dz}{\cos^2(z)} = 2i \tanh 1$, for every contour $\gamma$ in the domain of $\sec$ that starts at $-i$ and ends at $i$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
$\frac{d}{dz}\tan z = \sec^2 z = \frac{1}{\cos^2 z}$. Since $\tan z$ is a primitive, the integral is path independent and equals $\tan(i) - \tan(-i) = 2\tan(i) = 2i \tanh(1)$.
