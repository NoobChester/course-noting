### Prompt
Show that $\tan^{-1} w = \frac{1}{2i} \log \frac{1 + iw}{1 - iw}$ (as multi-valued functions). Define $\operatorname{PV}\tan^{-1} w = \frac{1}{2i} \operatorname{Log} \frac{1 + iw}{1 - iw}$.
Find all points $w$ where $\operatorname{PV}\tan^{-1} w$ is differentiable, and show that, when $|w| < 1$,
$$\operatorname{PV}\tan^{-1} w = w - \frac{w^3}{3} + \frac{w^5}{5} - \cdots.$$
