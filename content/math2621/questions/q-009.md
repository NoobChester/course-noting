### Prompt
Show that the triangle in the complex plane whose vertices are $z_1, z_2, z_3$ is equilateral if and only if $z_1^2 + z_2^2 + z_3^2 = z_1 z_2 + z_2 z_3 + z_3 z_1$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
The triangle is equilateral iff rotating side $z_2 - z_1$ by $\pi/3$ gives $z_3 - z_1$, i.e. $\frac{z_3 - z_1}{z_2 - z_1} = e^{\pm i\pi/3}$. Since $e^{i\pi/3}$ is a root of $\omega^2 - \omega + 1 = 0$, substituting $\omega = \frac{z_3 - z_1}{z_2 - z_1}$ and clearing denominators yields $z_1^2 + z_2^2 + z_3^2 - z_1 z_2 - z_2 z_3 - z_3 z_1 = 0$.
