Find two distinct Laurent expansions for $\frac{1}{z^2(1 - z)}$ in powers of $z$ and state the region of convergence of each expansion.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: $\sum_{n=-2}^\infty z^n$ when $0 < |z| < 1$, and $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{-3} (-1) z^n$ when $|z| > 1$.
