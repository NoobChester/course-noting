### Prompt
Define $f(z) = z \operatorname{Log}(z)$ when $z \ne 0$, and $f(0) = 0$. Is $f$ continuous or differentiable at $0$?

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: It is continuous but not differentiable (the difference quotient $\frac{f(z)-0}{z} = \operatorname{Log}(z)$ tends to $-\infty$ as $z \to 0$).
