### Prompt
Find the Laurent expansion of $\frac{1}{(z - 1)(z - 2)^2}$ that holds when $0 < |z-1| < 1$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Let $w = z-1$. Then $\frac{1}{w(w-1)^2} = \frac{1}{w} (1-w)^{-2} = \frac{1}{w} \sum_{n=0}^\infty (n+1) w^n = \sum_{n=-1}^\infty (n+2)(z-1)^n$.
