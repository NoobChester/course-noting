### Prompt
Suppose that $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n z^n$ and $g(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty b_n z^n$, and the series converge when $|z| < 2$. By integrating $f(z) g(z^{-1}) z^{-1}$ around the unit circle, show that
$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n b_n = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} f(e^{i\theta}) g(e^{-i\theta}) \, d\theta.$$
Deduce that $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n \bar{b}_n = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_0^{2\pi} f(e^{i\theta}) \overline{g(e^{i\theta})} \, d\theta$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
On the unit circle, $z = e^{i\theta}, dz = i e^{i\theta} d\theta$. $\frac{1}{2\pi i} \oint f(z) g(1/z) \frac{dz}{z} = \operatorname{Res}(f(z)g(1/z)/z, 0) = a_0 b_0 + a_1 b_1 + \dots = \sum a_n b_n$.
