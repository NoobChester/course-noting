### Prompt
Suppose that $f$ is an entire function, and that there is a (nonempty) open ball $B(w_0, r)$ such that $w \notin \operatorname{Range}(f)$ for all $w \in B(w_0, r)$. Show that $f$ is constant.
Hint: Consider the function $1/(f(z) - w_0)$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Since $f(z) \notin B(w_0, r)$, $|f(z) - w_0| \ge r > 0$ for all $z \in \mathbb{C}$. Thus $g(z) = \frac{1}{f(z) - w_0}$ has no poles in $\mathbb{C}$, so it is an entire function, and $|g(z)| \le 1/r$. By Liouville's theorem, $g$ is constant, hence $f$ is constant.
