### Prompt
Find the residues of each of the following functions at each of their singularities:
(a) $\frac{e^z}{z^2 + 1}$
(b) $\frac{\sinh z}{(z - i)^3}$
(c) $\frac{z^2 - 3z + 1}{(z^2 - 1)(z - 1)}$
(d) $\frac{e^z}{\cosh z}$
(e) $\tan z$

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: (a) $z = i, -i e^i/2; z = -i, i e^{-i}/2$; (b) $z = i, (i\sin 1)/2$; (c) $z = -1, 5/4; z = 1, -1/4$; (d) $z = i\pi/2 + k\pi i, 1$; (e) $z = \pi/2 + k\pi, -1$.
