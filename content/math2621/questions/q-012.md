### Prompt
Suppose that $w$ is an $n$th root of unity and $w \ne 1$. Show that $1 + w + w^2 + \cdots + w^{n-1} = 0$;
hence or otherwise, factorise $1 + z + z^2 + z^3 + z^4 + z^5$ over $\mathbb{C}$ and over $\mathbb{R}$.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Over $\mathbb{C}$: $(z - e^{i\pi/3})(z - e^{i2\pi/3})(z - e^{i\pi})(z - e^{i4\pi/3})(z - e^{i5\pi/3})$ = $(z+1)(z - e^{i\pi/3})(z - e^{-i\pi/3})(z - e^{i2\pi/3})(z - e^{-i2\pi/3})$. Over $\mathbb{R}$: $(z+1)(z^2 - z + 1)(z^2 + z + 1)$.
