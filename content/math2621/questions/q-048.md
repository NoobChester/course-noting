### Prompt
A subset $S$ of $\mathbb{C}$ is said to be arcwise connected if, given any two points $p$ and $q$ of $S$, there exists a continuous curve $\gamma : [0, 1] \to S$ such that $\gamma(0) = p$ and $\gamma(1) = q$. Show that if an open set is arcwise connected, then the continuous curve $\gamma$ may be taken to be polygonal.

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Cover the compact curve $\gamma([0,1])$ with finitely many open disks contained in $S$. In each disk, any two points can be connected by a straight line segment. Concatenating these segments produces a polygonal path in $S$ connecting $p$ and $q$.
