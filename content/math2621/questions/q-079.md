### Prompt
Evaluate the following integrals using the Cauchy Integral Formula (all contours traversed once anti-clockwise):
(a) $\int_{|z|=2} \frac{e^z}{z - 1} \, dz$
(b) $\int_{|z|=4} \frac{e^{2iz}}{(3z - 1)^2} \, dz$
(c) $\int_{|z|=2} \frac{z^2 + 1}{(z - 3)(z^2 - 1)} \, dz$
