### Prompt
Are the following sets open, closed, bounded, compact, polygonally path-connected, polygonally simply connected, regions or domains?
(a) $S_1 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| < 1\}$
(b) $S_2 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z| \le 1\}$
(c) $S_3 = \{z = x + iy \in \mathbb{C} : xy = 1\}$
(d) $S_4 = \{z = x + iy \in \mathbb{C} : xy > 1\}$
(e) $S_5 = \{z = x + iy \in \mathbb{C} : x \ge 0, y > 0\}$
(f) $S_6 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z + i| + |z - i| < 4\}$
(g) $S_7 = \{z \in \mathbb{C} : |z + i| + |z - i| = 4\}$
(h) $S_8 = \mathbb{C}$
(i) $S_9 = \emptyset$ (the empty set).

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
(a) Open, bounded, path-connected, simply connected domain. Not closed, not compact.
(b) Closed, bounded, compact, path-connected. Not open, not a domain.
(c) Closed, unbounded, disconnected (two branches $y = 1/x$).
(d) Open, unbounded, disconnected (first and third quadrants).
(f) Interior of ellipse: open, bounded, simply connected domain.
(g) Ellipse boundary: closed, bounded, compact curve.
