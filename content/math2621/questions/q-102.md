### Prompt
Locate and classify the singularities of the following functions:
(a) $\frac{1}{z^2 \sin z}$
(b) $\frac{z(z - \pi)^2}{\sin^2 z}$
(c) $\exp(z + 1/z)$
Evaluate the residues at each singularity of these functions (leave your answer to (c) as a series).

### Official Answer / Solution Notes
Answer: (a) $z = 0$: pole of order 3, residue 1/6 and $z = k\pi, k \ne 0$: simple poles, residue $(-1)^k (k\pi)^{-2}$. (b) $z = 0$: simple pole, residue $\pi^2$; $z = \pi$: removable singularity, residue 0; $z = k\pi, k \ne 0, 1$: double poles, residue $(3k^2 - 4k + 1)\pi^2$. (c) $z = 0$: essential singularity, residue $\sum_{k=0}^\infty \frac{1}{k!(k+1)!}$.
