# Question 1: Matrix Spectral Decomposition

Consider the real matrix:
$$A = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$$

1. Determine the characteristic polynomial $P(\lambda) = \det(A - \lambda I)$ and compute all eigenvalues.
2. For each eigenvalue, find an associated basis vector for the corresponding eigenspace.
3. Construct an invertible matrix $P$ and diagonal matrix $D$ such that $A = P D P^{-1}$.

## Solution

### Part 1: Characteristic Polynomial and Roots
We compute the determinant of $A - \lambda I$:

$$\det(A - \lambda I) = \begin{vmatrix} 4 - \lambda & 1 \\ 2 & 3 - \lambda \end{vmatrix} = (4 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda) - (1)(2)$$

Expanding the polynomial:
$$(4 - \lambda)(3 - \lambda) - 2 = \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 12 - 2 = \lambda^2 - 7\lambda + 10$$

Factoring gives:
$$P(\lambda) = (\lambda - 5)(\lambda - 2) = 0$$

Hence, the eigenvalues are:
$$\lambda_1 = 5, \quad \lambda_2 = 2$$

---

### Part 2: Associated Eigenvectors

#### For $\lambda_1 = 5$:
Solve $(A - 5I)\mathbf{v}_1 = \mathbf{0}$:
$$\begin{pmatrix} 4 - 5 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 - 5 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} -1 & 1 \\ 2 & -2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

Both rows yield $-x + y = 0 \implies y = x$. Choosing $x = 1$:
$$\mathbf{v}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$$

#### For $\lambda_2 = 2$:
Solve $(A - 2I)\mathbf{v}_2 = \mathbf{0}$:
$$\begin{pmatrix} 4 - 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 3 - 2 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

Row reduces to $2x + y = 0 \implies y = -2x$. Choosing $x = 1$:
$$\mathbf{v}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \end{pmatrix}$$

---

### Part 3: Diagonalization
Setting $P = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{v}_1 & \mathbf{v}_2 \end{pmatrix}$ and $D = \begin{pmatrix} \lambda_1 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_2 \end{pmatrix}$:

$$P = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -2 \end{pmatrix}, \quad D = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix}$$

Since $\det(P) = (1)(-2) - (1)(1) = -3 \neq 0$, $P$ is invertible and $A = P D P^{-1}$.
